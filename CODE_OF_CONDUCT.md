# Code of Conduct

Claude in Codex welcomes contributors regardless of background, identity, or experience. Treat other participants with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs, and boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant and protect other people's privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, and sharing someone else's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner, [Prashan Samarathunge](https://github.com/prashan-s), at [prashansamarathunge@gmail.com](mailto:prashansamarathunge@gmail.com). Avoid posting sensitive details or personal information in public issues. For an incident already visible in a GitHub discussion, GitHub's reporting tools are also available.

Maintainers review reports in context and may ask for clarification. They may remove content, close or lock discussions, warn participants, restrict participation temporarily, or ban participants for serious or repeated violations. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and its effect on the community.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to investigate or respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. The same expectations apply to maintainers.
